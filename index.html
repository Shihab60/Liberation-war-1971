Welcome to the Liberation War Archive of Bangladesh

The Liberation War Archive of Bangladesh is a vital resource for preserving the history and memories of the 1971 War of Liberation. It includes a wide range of materials such as treaties, declarations, manifestos, high-resolution photographs, and interviews with war heroes and affected individuals.
